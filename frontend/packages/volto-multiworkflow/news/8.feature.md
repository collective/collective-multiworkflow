Added support for the `workflow_states` key the backend serializes on content and on catalog summaries. `StateBadge` now also renders from a `workflow_states` value with an optional translated `label`, so a listing shows every item's states without requesting `@workflow` for each. New exports: the `getWorkflowStates`, `getAdditionalWorkflowStates`, `parseWorkflowState` and `formatWorkflowState` helpers, the `WORKFLOW_STATE_SEPARATOR` and `WORKFLOW_STATES_VOCABULARY` constants, and the `WorkflowStateValue`, `ParsedWorkflowState` and `WithWorkflowStates` types. @ericof
