Added an optional `label` attribute to the `<plone:additionalworkflows />` directive, naming the workflow in the user interface in place of its title. The label is translatable, in the `i18n_domain` of the ZCML file declaring it: the `@workflow` endpoint reports it as the chain entry's `title`, and the vocabulary of the Review state collection criterion names the workflow's states with it. A directive with a `label` must list exactly one workflow; without one, the workflow's title is used as before. @ericof
